# Privacy Policy — Sonos Lyrics Local

_Last updated: 2026-07-29_

Sonos Lyrics Local ("the app") is an Android app that displays the album art and
lyrics of music currently playing on Sonos speakers on your local network.

## Information the app itself collects

The app does **not** operate its own servers and does **not** collect, store, or
transmit any personal information to the developer. All Sonos discovery and
playback reading happens locally on your Wi‑Fi network between your device and
your speakers.

To show lyrics, the app sends the **artist, track title, album, and track
duration** of the currently playing song to third‑party lyrics services
(lrclib.net and lyrics.ovh). No account, device identifier, or personal
information is included in those requests.

## Advertising

The app displays ads through **Google AdMob**. To provide and measure ads,
Google's advertising SDK may collect and process data as described in Google's
policies, which can include:

- A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID).
- Approximate, non‑precise information such as general location derived from IP
  address, and device/app interaction data.

This data is used to serve and measure ads and, where you consent, to
personalize them. In regions where required (e.g. the EEA/UK), the app presents
a consent form on first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can
choose non‑personalized ads.

- How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob/Google Ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — to reach your speakers and fetch lyrics and ads.
- **Wi‑Fi state / multicast** — to discover Sonos speakers on your network.
- **Advertising ID** — used by the ads SDK as described above.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information
from children.
